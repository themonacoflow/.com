Operations

From your purchase order to a live, MAP-compliant listing.

Nothing here is unusual. It is the ordinary wholesale process, documented, so your channel and brand-protection teams know exactly what happens to your product after it leaves your dock.

Chain of custody

Six steps · every unit, every time

01 — Purchase order

We issue a purchase order to you or to a distributor you have authorized, at the pricing and minimums you set. First orders are prepaid. Terms, if any, come later and in writing.

02 — Inbound

Freight ships to our fulfillment partner, a licensed third-party logistics facility in Newark, Delaware, with a dock for carton and pallet inbound. Inbound instructions, appointment requirements, and the receiving address are issued once the PO is confirmed.

03 — Inspection

Receipts are counted against the packing list and checked for damage, correct case pack, and date or lot codes. Short shipments and damage are reported back to you with photos, not absorbed silently.

04 — Prep and labeling

Units are prepped to marketplace specification: polybagging where required, bundling for multipacks, protective packaging for fragile goods, and the marketplace item label applied. Your packaging, branding, and inserts stay intact.

05 — Forward to channel

A shipment plan is created and cartons are forwarded to the assigned Amazon fulfillment centers, or to another marketplace or channel where that is the better route for the line.

06 — Listed and replenished

Product goes live under our seller account at or above MAP, on brand-compliant listing content, and is reordered against actual sell-through.

Each step leaves a document behind — a PO, an invoice, a receiving report, a shipment plan. That is what makes a wholesale relationship auditable instead of a matter of trust.

Standing rules

The parts brands actually ask about.

Pricing and MAP

We list at or above your posted MAP and hold that price through promotions, holiday periods, and competitive pressure. If a marketplace mechanism moves our price below MAP, we pull the offer rather than let it sit there.

Send us the MAP sheet and the policy that governs it. If your policy requires an authorized-reseller agreement or a letter of authorization, we will sign it.

Listing content

We sell on your existing listings wherever they exist and follow your content, image, and claim guidelines as written. Where a listing needs to be created, we build it from the material you provide and hand the content back to you for correction.

Replenishment

Reorders are driven by sell-through and inbound lead time, not by guesswork, so you get a repeating order pattern rather than one opportunistic buy. We will tell you the cadence we expect to run before the first PO, and update you as real data arrives.

Returns and defects

Marketplace customer returns come back into the channel, are graded, and are either relisted as sellable or removed. Unsellable units are our cost, not yours.

Where a return pattern points at a manufacturing or packaging defect, we report it to you with the return reasons and photos. We do not send goods back to you without agreeing it first.

Documentation on request

Our W-9, resale certificate where applicable, the seller name we operate under, and copies of the purchase invoices behind the units in question — provided to your brand-protection or channel team on request.

Categories we stay out of

We do not list into marketplace categories we are not approved for, and we do not take on goods whose handling requirements we cannot meet at our fulfillment partner. If a line needs a certification we do not hold, we will say so instead of trying it.

Send the catalog and the MAP sheet.

We will come back with the SKUs we can move, the cadence we expect, and any questions your compliance team should ask us first.
