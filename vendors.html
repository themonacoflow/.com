Vendor inquiry

Reaches the team directly

Sends straight to the team. We publish no email address on this site, so nothing here can be scraped — if you would rather talk, call (877) 460-7547, Mon–Fri 9–5 ET.

What happens next

No portal, no drip sequence, no sales call you did not ask for.

What we need
A line sheet or catalog with wholesale pricing and case packs, your MAP policy if one exists, and whoever handles your channel decisions.
What we do with it
We check the categories against what we run, look at current marketplace listings and who is on them, and work out whether the numbers survive marketplace fees at your MAP.
What you get back
A direct answer: the SKUs we would start with, the order size, the replenishment cadence we expect, and the pricing we will list at. If it is not a fit, we will tell you that instead, with the reason.
If it is a fit
We sign your reseller agreement or LOA if you require one, send our W-9 and resale certificate where applicable, and place a prepaid opening order.
Your information
Used to evaluate and respond to your inquiry, and nothing else. See the privacy policy.

Rather talk it through first?

Call during business hours and you will get someone who can answer questions about sourcing, prep, and pricing without a handoff.
