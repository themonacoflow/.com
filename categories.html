Fit

What makes a line worth taking on.

We would rather tell you no quickly than run your product badly. These are the things we look at first.

It reorders
Products that a customer buys again, or that sell through a season predictably. One-time novelty items do not build a channel.
There is a pricing policy
A written MAP policy, or a willingness to set one. Without a floor, everyone in your channel races to the bottom and your brand pays for it.
The economics survive the channel
Wholesale cost, case pack, weight and dimensions, and marketplace fees have to leave room for both of us at your MAP. We will run those numbers before we ask you for a PO.
Packaging survives transit
Retail packaging that arrives intact after a parcel network, or that we can protect at prep without covering your branding.
You want the channel managed
The relationship works when a brand wants fewer, accountable sellers. If your plan is to open every account that applies, we are not the right fit.
What we pass on
Temperature-controlled goods, hazardous materials, and anything whose handling or certification requirements we cannot meet at our fulfillment partner. We will say so instead of trying it.

Not sure your line fits?

Send the catalog anyway. A short answer from us costs you one email and saves a quarter of guessing.
