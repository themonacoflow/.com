About

A wholesale company that owns what it sells.

Monaco Flow LLC is a Florida limited liability company, registered in 2025 and with a mailing address in Orlando. We buy manufacturer lines at wholesale, take ownership of the inventory, and sell it on Amazon and other U.S. marketplaces under the brand’s pricing rules.

What we do

Most brands reach a point where marketplace demand exists but running the channel in-house is a distraction: the listings, the prep, the inbound shipments, the replenishment math, the unauthorized sellers. We take the purchasing risk and run that channel as a wholesale customer — we pay for the goods, hold the inventory, and answer for how it is sold.

We buy direct from manufacturers and from distributors those manufacturers authorize. Product ships to our fulfillment partner in Newark, Delaware, where it is received, inspected, prepped, and forwarded into Amazon or another channel. It goes live at or above MAP and is reordered against sell-through.

How we operate

We run deliberately narrow. It is better for a brand to have one reseller who moves a defined set of SKUs at a predictable cadence, honors the pricing policy, and answers the phone, than a long tail of accounts nobody can identify. That is the position we are trying to hold.

Orlando is our mailing address, not a facility. Goods never go there. Fulfillment runs through a licensed third-party logistics facility in Newark, Delaware — a professional operation with a dock, carton and pallet inbound, marketplace prep, and forwarding. We do not operate a warehouse of our own, and the Orlando address is not one.

What we are not

  • Not a liquidator. We are not looking for closeouts, overstock dumps, or distressed inventory to flip.
  • Not a broker. We buy the goods and own them. There is no third party behind us taking title.
  • Not a gray-market seller. If we cannot show you where a unit came from, we do not sell it.
  • Not a discounter. Winning a sale by undercutting your posted price costs you more than the sale is worth.
