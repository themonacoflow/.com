Privacy policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy covers themonacoflow.com, operated by Monaco Flow LLC (“Monaco Flow”, “we”, “us”). This is a business-to-business site. We do not sell to consumers here and we do not run a store on this domain.

What we collect

Only what you send us. If you submit a vendor inquiry, use the assistant, or call, that is typically:

  • Your name, title, business email, and phone number
  • The brand or company you represent and its website or catalog link
  • Business details you choose to include — product lines, pricing, MAP policy, marketplace presence, and similar

The vendor form sends your submission straight to our team as email. It is not stored on this site or in any database, and this site publishes no email address for a crawler to harvest.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry
  • To evaluate whether a wholesale relationship makes sense
  • To administer purchase orders, documentation, and the ongoing account if we open one

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

Who else sees it

Our email and business software providers process it in the course of providing those services. We share information with accounting, tax, logistics, or legal providers only where it is needed to carry out the business relationship or to meet a legal obligation.

The assistant

The chat assistant on this site is an AI. What you type is sent to our hosting provider and to xAI, which generates the reply, and a transcript of the conversation is emailed to our team so a person can follow up. It sets no cookie and does not identify you. Please do not type anything confidential into it — use the vendor form or the phone for that.

Cookies, analytics, and fonts

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and it embeds no third-party chat widget. It does load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google’s servers and Google receives the request as it would for any other third-party asset.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as it is useful to the business relationship, and longer where tax, accounting, or legal requirements apply. Ask us to delete an inquiry that did not lead anywhere and we will.

Your requests

Use the contact form, call (877) 460-7547, or write to the postal address below to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will respond within a reasonable period and, where a specific privacy law applies to you, within the period that law requires.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a company of our size. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive personal information through this site.

Children

This site is directed at businesses. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
