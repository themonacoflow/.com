Chain of custody

Purchase order → live listing

  1. 01

    Purchase order

    We issue a PO to the manufacturer or one of its authorized distributors and pay on prepayment or approved terms.

  2. 02

    Inbound

    Cartons or pallets are delivered to our fulfillment partner’s dock in Newark, Delaware.

  3. 03

    Inspection

    Receipts are counted against the packing list and checked for condition, case pack, and date or lot codes.

  4. 04

    Prep

    Units are prepped to marketplace specification — polybag, labeling, bundling — and marked with the marketplace identifier.

  5. 05

    Forward

    A shipment plan is created and cartons are forwarded to the assigned Amazon fulfillment center or another channel.

  6. 06

    Listed

    Product goes live under our seller account at or above MAP, then is replenished against sell-through.

Every unit we sell traces back to an invoice from your company or a distributor you authorize. Your brand-protection team can ask for that paperwork at any time and get it.

How we work

Three things a brand can hold us to.

Wholesale goes wrong in predictable ways: unverifiable supply, sloppy handling, and broken pricing. These are the commitments that keep those from happening on your line.

Supply

Authorized supply

We buy direct from manufacturers and authorized distributors. Every unit moves on a real invoice, and we hand that documentation over on request — no diverted product, no unverifiable lots.

Fulfillment

Professional fulfillment

Freight goes to a licensed third-party logistics facility in Newark, Delaware, which receives, inspects, preps, and forwards to Amazon. Our Orlando address takes mail only; goods never go there.

Discipline

Channel discipline

We list at or above MAP, follow brand guidelines as written, and stay out of categories we are not approved for. No gray market, no MAP breaks, no surprises on your Buy Box.
